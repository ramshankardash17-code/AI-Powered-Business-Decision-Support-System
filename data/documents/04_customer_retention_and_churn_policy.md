# Customer Retention & Churn Intervention Policy (SOP-CRM-2024-V4)

## 1. High-Value / High-Churn Identification Matrix
Customers are categorized based on their dual-score positioning:
- **Predicted Future CLV**: Customers in the upper 25th percentile of monetary lifetime value (> 15,000 currency units).
- **ML Churn Probability**: Customers with evaluated churn probability exceeding **70.0%**.

## 2. Retention Intervention Protocols
When a customer is flagged as **HIGH VALUE + HIGH CHURN RISK**:
1. **Automated Concierge Outreach**: If inactivity was triggered by a delivery delay (> 6 days) or unresolved complaint, an automated high-priority service recovery ticket is opened.
2. **Value Protection Offer**: The retention engine is authorized to generate a personalized 15% discretionary re-engagement code or complimentary shipping for 90 days.
3. **Escalation Boundary**: Autonomous marketing scripts cannot issue discounts exceeding 20% or credit cash directly without Human Retention Manager authorization.
