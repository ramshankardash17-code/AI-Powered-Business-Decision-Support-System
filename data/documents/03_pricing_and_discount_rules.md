# Pricing Strategy & Promotional Discount Governance (POL-PRC-2024)

## 1. Margin Floor Restrictions
To maintain sustainable EBITDA margins, all retail discounting must adhere to mandatory category margin floors:
- **Electronics**: Minimum gross margin floor of **8.0%**. No promotional campaign may discount items below supplier cost plus fulfillment expense without VP Commercial approval.
- **Apparel**: Margin floor of **25.0%**. End-of-season clearance allowed up to 45% discount.
- **Home & Kitchen**: Margin floor of **18.0%**.
- **Beauty & Personal Care**: Margin floor of **30.0%**.

## 2. Customer Tier Preferential Discounts
- **Champions & VIPs**: Automatic 10% coupon issued biannually; eligible for complimentary express delivery on all orders.
- **Discount Seekers**: Targeted exclusively with slow-moving inventory bundles to clear warehouse space while safeguarding regular full-price catalog margins.
