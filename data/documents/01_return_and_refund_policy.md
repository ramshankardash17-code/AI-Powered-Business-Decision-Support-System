# Enterprise Return and Refund Policy (SOP-RET-2024-V3)

## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy
Our enterprise is committed to customer satisfaction while maintaining margin health. This Standard Operating Procedure governs the return, exchange, and refund workflows across all retail categories.

## 2. Category-Specific Return Windows
- **Electronics & Appliances**: Customers may initiate returns within **14 calendar days** from the verified delivery timestamp. Items must include all original packaging, serialized barcodes, accessories, and unactivated warranty cards. Opened software or items with tampered serial numbers are strictly non-returnable.
- **Apparel, Footwear & Accessories**: Returns accepted within **30 calendar days** from delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with all original tags attached.
- **Beauty & Personal Care**: For hygiene reasons, opened or unsealed beauty products are non-returnable unless arrived damaged or defective upon delivery.
- **Home & Kitchen**: Standard **21 calendar day** return window. Damaged or missing component claims must be reported within 48 hours.

## 3. High Return Rate Protocol & Supplier Accountability
When an individual product line exhibits a return rate exceeding **12.0%** over a rolling 30-day window, the Product Operations and Quality Assurance teams must initiate an immediate batch investigation:
1. The supplier is notified within 24 hours to provide defect diagnostic reports.
2. If the return rate exceeds **18.0%**, the SKU is temporarily deactivated from the storefront until supplier remediation is approved.
3. Supplier contracts stipulate that defect-driven returns will be debited directly against accounts payable.

## 4. Refund Processing & Payment Channels
- **UPI & Debit Cards**: Refunds credited within 2 to 4 business days post-warehouse quality inspection.
- **Credit Cards**: Refunds posted within 3 to 7 business days depending on the card issuer.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD)**: Customer provides NEFT/IMPS bank coordinates; funds transferred within 3 business days.
